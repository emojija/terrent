Terret Jewellery ERP Billing Software

Our Jewellery Billing Software is designed to make billing and inventory management simple for jewellery stores. It helps with quick and GST-compliant invoicing, barcode scanning, weight-based billing, and real-time gold & silver rate updates. You can track customer purchases, manage stock, generate reports, and even offer EMI options. The software supports multi-user access, digital receipts, and secure data backups.

Find Better Terret ERP Billing Software Features

HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification)

Hallmark Unique Identification is a 6-digit alphanumeric code assigned to each hallmarked gold item by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).

Payment & Reconciliation

Payment management in jewelry ERP software helps businesses handle customer payments, track due amounts, and process transactions smoothly.

GST Billing & Return Filing

GST billing makes it easy for jewelry businesses to create tax-compliant invoices with the correct GST rates. It automatically calculates taxes, so there’s no manual effort or mistakes.

Barcode Management

Barcode management helps jewelry businesses keep track of their stock easily. Each piece of jewelry gets a unique barcode, which can be scanned to check details like price, weight, and availability.

Sales Report

The Sales Report module in ERP Jewellery Billing Software provides detailed insights into sales performance, revenue trends, and customer purchasing behavior.

Stock Entry

The Stock Entry module in ERP Jewellery Billing Software helps manage inventory efficiently by recording the addition of new stock, tracking existing inventory, and ensuring accurate valuation..

Inventory Management

Inventory management helps jewelry businesses keep track of their stock in real time. It records every piece of jewelry, from raw materials to finished products, ensuring nothing gets lost or misplaced.

Order Book

An Order Book ERP software tailored for jewelry businesses streamlines the entire order management process. It tracks orders from customer request to delivery, including inventory management, pricing, invoicing, and customer communication.

Get Order

Getting orders in the jewelry business involves creating a strong presence both online and offline to reach your target audience effectively.
